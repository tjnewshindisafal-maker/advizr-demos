Good to know

What should I do for a sudden toothache?

Contact us immediately for an emergency appointment; avoid self-medicating and apply a cold compress.

How often should I visit the dentist for a check-up?

Generally, a check-up and professional cleaning every six months is recommended for optimal oral health.

Are dental bridges a painful procedure?

The procedure is typically performed under local anesthetic to ensure your comfort throughout the process.
